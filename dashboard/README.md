**Ireland Housing Market Intelligence**

Subtitle: **Residential Property Prices, Housing Supply & Regional Market Trends**

## Reporting period

## Power BI input files

- `market_intelligence_quarterly.csv`

## Key DAX measures

```DAX
Latest National Completions =
VAR LatestQuarter =
    MAX(market_intelligence_quarterly[Quarter Start])
RETURN
    CALCULATE(
        MAX(market_intelligence_quarterly[ireland_completions]),
        market_intelligence_quarterly[Quarter Start] = LatestQuarter
    )
```

## Final visuals

- Five KPI cards for national completions, price growth and completion growth.
- Reporting-quarter card.
- Line chart: annual property-price growth, Dublin vs outside Dublin.
- Line chart: quarterly dwelling completions, Dublin vs outside Dublin.
- Horizontal bar chart: Top 10 local authorities by latest-quarter completions.
- Donut chart: latest national completions by house type.

## Validation notes

The two line charts use the direct quarter-start date rather than Power BI's automatic Date Hierarchy. Price-growth series are displayed at quarterly grain using Average aggregation, while completion series use Sum at the same quarterly grain. Both comparison series share one y-axis within each chart.
