## Long-term price observations

Within the selected headline annual-growth series, Dublin recorded the strongest observation at approximately **27.9% in September 2014** and the weakest at approximately **-28.1% in June 2009**.

## Interpretation guardrails

The combined dataset is descriptive. It does not establish that changes in completions cause changes in property prices. The latest quarter is a useful example: Dublin completions fell year-on-year while Dublin price growth was weaker than outside Dublin, whereas outside Dublin recorded both stronger price growth and positive completion growth.
